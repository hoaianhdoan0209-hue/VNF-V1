# VNF V1 — Living Fantasy World Specification

V1 is not a version-number rename. It is the first release that must feel like a continuous living world.

## Non-negotiable invariants
- WORLD FIRST: simulation state is truth; language and presentation may read it but may not invent history.
- Haru is autonomous. God/System may observe, explain, teach, propose bounded world conditions and repair safe technical state, but never directly rewrites Haru's personality, emotion, memory, relationship or intention.
- Active and Offline are one causal life.
- Visible behavior must be derived from body, cognition, environment and history rather than random presentation scripts.
- Real-world knowledge is an inspiration/constraint layer, not copied world truth. VNF transforms real concepts into fictional equivalents with provenance.
- Fictional fauna must not be direct copies of real animal species.

## V1 pillars
1. Biological Haru: respiration, circulation/metabolism, digestion/hydration, endocrine, thermal regulation, skin/immune load, localized pain, sleep/recovery, musculoskeletal and nervous constraints.
2. Physics: SI gravity, mass, acceleration, collisions, traction, slope, falling, impact and terrain-grounding.
3. Camera: automatic cat-POV framing that follows physical elevation, travel, rest, encounters and God manifestation without manual world-control.
4. Living ecology: persistent fictional creatures, living flora, biome fields, resource pressure, migration, activity cycles and environmental response.
5. Atmosphere/weather: pressure, gas composition, temperature, humidity, air quality, density, wind/cloud/rain and local elevation/biome coupling.
6. Visual world: layered environment art, water, weather, lighting, foreground/midground/background depth and state-driven organism motion.
7. God presence: non-human in-world manifestation. World remains visible. God knowledge is broader than Haru knowledge; God does not become Haru's controller.
8. World Library: extensible source-backed knowledge anchors inspired by current human knowledge (including Wikimedia/Wikidata-style open knowledge graphs), transformed through explicit fictionalization rules before becoming authored VNF content.

## Knowledge separation
- System/God library: broad reference knowledge + provenance.
- Authored world definition: committed fictional reality.
- Haru knowledge: only learned from lived perception, teaching or evidence allowed by the simulation.
- Memory/history: only events that actually happened on the causal timeline.

## V1 acceptance gate
V1.0 may be released only when:
- signed self-update works from the prior installed release without deleting save data;
- physics/gravity and active/offline parity tests pass;
- Haru body systems remain stable under ordinary daily activity and visibly affect behavior;
- all four launch biomes visibly contain state-driven flora or fictional fauna;
- atmosphere/weather affects both world visuals and bodies;
- God appears as an in-world non-human presence and remains autonomy-safe;
- World Library provenance and fantasy-transformation rules are inspectable;
- graphics use production layered assets rather than placeholder geometry for primary scenes.
